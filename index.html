Happy Birthday, Adam.
a letter from someone who loves you very much

Dear Adam,

Happy birthday to the person who somehow became such a big part of my little world.

I honestly don't know how to start this because there are so many things I want to say to you. I could probably write pages and pages about you and still feel like I forgot something.

So maybe I'll start with the simplest thing.

I'm really, really glad that you exist.

I'm glad that somewhere in this huge world, our paths crossed. I'm glad I got to know you. I'm glad I got to hear your stories, your random thoughts, your silly jokes, and all the little things that slowly made you become someone so special to me.

Sometimes I still find it funny how a person can slowly become part of your everyday life without you even realising it.

At first, you are just someone you talk to. Then you start looking for their messages. Then you start wanting to tell them everything. Then suddenly, their name becomes one of the names your heart looks for without even asking.

And somehow, that person became you.

Adam, I want you to know that I appreciate you. More than I probably say out loud.

I appreciate the little things you do. I appreciate your time. I appreciate every conversation, every laugh, every stupid moment, every random memory, and even the moments that may seem completely ordinary to you.

Because sometimes the ordinary moments are the ones I end up remembering the most.

I also want to say I'm sorry.

I'm sorry for the times I misunderstood you. I'm sorry for the times I didn't explain myself properly. I'm sorry for the moments when my emotions became louder than my words. I'm sorry if there were times when I made you question how much you mean to me.

I know I'm not perfect. I know loving someone also means learning how to understand them, communicate with them, and sometimes put our pride aside.

And I'm still learning.

I'm learning how to love you better. I'm learning how to communicate better. I'm learning that sometimes I don't need to overthink every little thing. Sometimes I just need to tell you what is in my heart instead of letting my fears create stories inside my head.

Because if there is one thing I am scared of, it is losing you.

I don't think I've ever been good at admitting how scared I am of losing someone I love.

Sometimes I act like I'm okay. Sometimes I pretend that I don't care too much. Sometimes I make jokes about things that actually hurt me.

But the truth is, I care. A lot.

Maybe a little too much sometimes.

And that is probably why I overthink. Why I get scared. Why I worry about small things. Why sometimes I become quiet when I actually have a thousand things I want to say.

I think the scary thing about loving someone is knowing that they can become so important to you.

Suddenly their happiness matters to you. Their sadness matters to you. Their silence matters to you. Their absence feels different. And their presence can make an ordinary day feel a little softer.

That's what you became to me.

Something soft in my life. Something I don't want to lose.

And Adam, if sometimes I seem scared, please know that it isn't because I don't trust what we have.

It's because I know how precious it is to me.

I know people change. Life changes. Circumstances change. We grow. We learn. We become different versions of ourselves.

And maybe that's what scares me.

The thought that one day something that feels so familiar could become a memory.

I don't want you to become just a name I once knew.

I don't want our silly memories to become something I can only look back at.

I don't want to wake up one day and realise that the person I used to tell everything to is now someone I don't talk to anymore.

Maybe that sounds a little sad for a birthday letter.

But I wanted to be honest with you.

Because behind all the birthday wishes, all the cute things, and all the smiles, there is also a heart that genuinely hopes you stay in my life for a very, very long time.

I hope this isn't just another birthday we remember. I hope there are many more.

I hope I get to wish you happy birthday again next year. And the year after that. And the year after that.

I hope I get to watch you grow. I hope I get to see you achieve the things you always wanted. I hope I get to celebrate your little victories with you.

And when life gets difficult, I hope you remember that there is someone who genuinely wants to see you okay.

Someone who wants you to be happy. Someone who is proud of you. Someone who believes that you deserve good things.

I hope you never forget how loved you are.

Not only by me, but by the people who raised you, the people who know you, and the people whose lives you've touched.

Speaking of that...

I really love seeing the little Adam from those childhood pictures.

It's kind of cute to think that the little boy in those pictures grew up to become the person standing in front of me today.

And somehow, I wish I could tell that little Adam one thing.

"You are going to become someone very loved."

And maybe one day, if I get the chance, I would love to meet the people who raised you and thank them too.

Thank them for raising you. Thank them for taking care of you. Thank them for helping you become the person I know today.

I know I can never understand every part of your life or everything that made you who you are.

But I want to keep learning.

I want to know your stories. Your childhood stories. Your dreams. Your fears. The things that make you happy. The things that annoy you. The little things you don't usually tell people.

I want to know you, not just the version of you that everyone sees, but the quiet parts too.

And if you ever have a bad day, I hope you feel safe enough to tell me.

You don't always have to be strong.

You don't always have to pretend you're okay.

You can be tired. You can be sad. You can be confused. You can have bad days.

I hope you know that being loved doesn't mean you have to be perfect all the time.

And I hope I can remember that too.

I know there will be days when we misunderstand each other. There will probably be moments when we annoy each other. There may be times when we don't know what to say.

But I hope we learn to choose communication instead of silence.

I hope we learn to say sorry when we are wrong. I hope we learn to forgive. I hope we learn to understand instead of assuming.

And most importantly, I hope we keep choosing each other.

Not because everything is always easy, but because what we have is worth taking care of.

Adam, I don't know what the future looks like.

I don't know where life will take us. I don't know what kind of people we will become.

But I do know one thing.

Right now, I am grateful that I have you.

And I don't want to take that for granted.

So on your birthday, I hope you receive more than presents.

I hope you receive peace. I hope you receive happiness. I hope you receive opportunities. I hope your dreams slowly become real. I hope your heart stays soft. I hope you are always surrounded by people who genuinely care about you.

And I hope, somehow, I get to be one of those people for a very long time.

Thank you for being you.

Thank you for existing.

Thank you for coming into my life.

Thank you for every laugh, every conversation, every memory, every silly moment, and every little piece of happiness you've given me.

I'm sorry for the moments where I made things harder than they needed to be.

I'm sorry for the times my fears got in the way.

I'm sorry for every time I made you feel less loved than you actually are.

I'm still learning. But I hope you can see that my heart is sincere.

And if I could wish for something selfish today, it would be this:

please stay.

Stay for the random conversations. Stay for the stupid jokes. Stay for the late nights. Stay for the birthdays. Stay for the little celebrations. Stay for the days when everything feels perfect. And stay for the days when everything feels messy.

I want more memories with you.

More pictures. More laughter. More places. More food. More random conversations. More "remember when..." stories.

More ordinary days that somehow become special just because you're there.

I want to look back one day and realise that we really did have so many beautiful memories together.

And maybe years from now, when we look at these pictures, we'll laugh at how young we were.

Maybe we'll look at this website and say, "why was this so dramatic?"

And honestly... I hope we get that chance.

Happy birthday, Adam Hakimi.

May this new year of your life be gentle with you. May you grow into everything you dream of becoming. May you always have reasons to smile. May your heart be protected from things that hurt you. And may you always know that you are worth loving.

I hope today reminds you how special you are.

And if nobody tells you enough, let me tell you today.

You are precious to me.

More than you probably realise.

So here's to another year of you. Another year of growing. Another year of learning. Another year of becoming the person you're meant to be.

And hopefully, another year of us.

I love you.

And I'm sorry if sometimes I don't know how to show it properly.

I'm trying.

And I hope you can feel it, even in the little things.

Happy birthday, my favourite person.

Please keep smiling.

Please keep being you.

And please remember that somewhere in this world, there is a girl who is very grateful that she got to meet you.

That girl is me.

And she hopes she gets to stay beside you for a very long time.

with all my love,
Umairah ♡
